# Briefing on the Royal Commission into the Robodebt Scheme

The Royal Commission into the Robodebt Scheme (Australia) reported in July 2023.
Official report: https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/
The report text is not reproduced. This briefing is original and is licensed CC0. It records publicly reported findings for use as a global example.

## Automated debts
<!-- tags: D9 D10 | nodes: M P SINK | factor: research | domain: gov_services -->

Robodebt used automated income averaging to raise social-security debts. The Royal Commission found the scheme was unlawful. Debts were asserted against people without a sufficient legal basis and without a meaningful human check of the individual file before the debt was raised. The harm was not a rare model error. It was a system that treated an averaged figure as if it were proof.

## What later programmes should take from it
<!-- tags: D10 D8 | nodes: P | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

The global lesson is domain-level. An assistant that denies a benefit or asserts a debt needs a cited legal basis, an explanation a person can use, and a human review path that can stop the decision. Publishing a model card after the fact does not supply that path.
