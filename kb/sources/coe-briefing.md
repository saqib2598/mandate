# Briefing on the Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence

The Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law (CETS No. 225) was opened for signature in 2024.
URL: https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-framework-convention-on-artificial-intelligence
The treaty text is not reproduced. This briefing is original and is licensed CC0.

## Human dignity and remedies
<!-- tags: D10 D8 | nodes: P SINK | factor: standards | domain: cross -->

The Framework Convention requires parties to address risks to human rights, democracy and the rule of law across the lifecycle of an AI system. For a public-service assistant that denies a benefit, that means a remedy a person can actually use, not only an internal log. Transparency and accountability are duties of the deployer, including when a vendor hosts the model.

## Safe innovation
<!-- tags: D11 D10 | nodes: Sandbox | factor: sandbox | domain: cross -->

The Convention leaves room for sandboxes so that public bodies can test a system before it touches a live decision. A sandbox is not a substitute for a mandate. It is where a refusal, a breach notification, and a human-review path can be exercised without spending public money on a live mistake.
