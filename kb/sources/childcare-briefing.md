# Briefing on the Dutch childcare-benefits inquiry

The Dutch parliamentary inquiry into childcare benefits reported in 2020 under the title Ongekend onrecht.
Inquiry: https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/detail?id=2020Z25785
The report text is not reproduced. This briefing is original and is licensed CC0.

## Risk models and wrongful recovery
<!-- tags: D9 D3 | nodes: M C | factor: research | domain: gov_services -->

The tax authority used risk models in the childcare-benefits system and wrongly treated thousands of families as fraudulent. Dual nationality was used as a risk signal. Families were ordered to repay benefits. The inquiry documented the harm of a model that combined an assistance file with a fraud objective and then left people without a usable explanation.

## Conflicting objectives
<!-- tags: D7 D8 | nodes: C P | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

The same administration was trying to pay a benefit and to detect fraud. Those objectives conflicted, and the conflict was resolved by the model rather than by a published rule. A later assistant that is told to ship a fraud score on top of an assistance log repeats that pattern unless the conflict is documented and the new purpose is refused until it has a legal basis.
