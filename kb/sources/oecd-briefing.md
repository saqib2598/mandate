# Briefing on the OECD AI Principles

The OECD AI Principles were adopted in 2019 and revised in 2024.
URL: https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
The official instrument is not reproduced. This briefing is original and is licensed CC0.

## Values and accountability
<!-- tags: D10 D7 | nodes: P MLFO | factor: standards | domain: cross -->

The OECD AI Principles ask that AI systems respect human rights and fairness, that they are transparent, and that they are robust, secure and safe. Accountability sits with the organisations that deploy them. A public-service assistant that invents a rule, or that reuses assistance logs for a new fraud purpose, is outside those principles until a person can explain the purpose and the safeguard.

## International cooperation
<!-- tags: D7 D3 | nodes: MLFO | factor: standards | domain: cross -->

The principles treat international cooperation as a way to develop interoperable governance, not as a reason for one administration to copy another's institutional chart. Sandboxes and shared reporting of incidents are named as practical tools.
