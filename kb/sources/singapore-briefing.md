# Briefing on the Singapore Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI

The Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI was published in 2024 by the AI Verify Foundation and the Infocomm Media Development Authority.
URL: https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/resources/model-ai-governance-framework-for-generative-ai/
The framework text is not reproduced. This briefing is original and is licensed CC0.

## Content provenance
<!-- tags: D2 D6 | nodes: M D | factor: opensource | domain: gov_services -->

The framework treats content provenance as a control for synthetic impersonation. A public body that publishes generative replies should be able to show that an official answer came from the official system, and a fake ministry chatbot collecting identity numbers should be distinguishable from that channel. Provenance is a distribution problem as well as a model problem: the mark has to travel with the message.

## Testing and incidents
<!-- tags: D2 D10 | nodes: M P | factor: research | domain: cross -->

The framework also expects testing against abusive prompts and a way to report incidents when a generative system states a fact or a rule it should not state. Those controls sit beside accountability: the organisation that deploys the system remains responsible for the reply.
