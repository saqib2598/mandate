# Briefing on the SyRI judgment

The Hague District Court, 5 February 2020, ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2020:1878, stopped the Dutch SyRI legislation.
Judgment: https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/details?id=ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2020:1878
The judgment text is not reproduced. This briefing is original and is licensed CC0.

## Risk scores for social-security fraud
<!-- tags: D3 D10 | nodes: SRC P | factor: data | domain: data_protection -->

SyRI was a system for linking public-service data and scoring neighbourhoods and people for a risk of social-security fraud. The court held that the legislation did not meet Article 8 of the European Convention on Human Rights. The interference with private life was not proportionate, and the system was not transparent enough for a person to understand or to challenge how a risk score was produced.

## Purpose and the source of the data
<!-- tags: D3 D7 | nodes: SRC C | factor: data | domain: gov_services -->

Data collected to administer a benefit were drawn into a fraud score. That change of purpose, hidden inside a cross-domain collector, is the part of the case later programmes still trip over. A new score needs its own legal basis. An instruction to reuse the logs does not create one.
