## What a record contains
<!-- tags: D10 D7 | nodes: P SINK | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

The Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard asks public bodies to publish a record of an algorithmic tool that supports a decision. The record names the tool, the decision it supports, the data it uses, the points where a person reviews the output, and a contact for someone affected by the decision. Publishing the record does not itself authorise the decision.

## Limits of the record
<!-- tags: D10 | nodes: P | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

A transparency record is not a mandate. It does not grant a power to deny a benefit, and it does not replace an explanation to the person who was refused. It tells the public that the tool exists and how a human remains in the decision.
