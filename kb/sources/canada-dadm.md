## Section 4 Objectives
<!-- tags: D10 D7 | nodes: P | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

The objective of this directive is to ensure that automated decision systems are used in a manner that reduces risks to clients, departments and Canadian society, and leads to more efficient, accurate, consistent and interpretable decisions made pursuant to Canadian law. Decisions made by departments are to be data-driven and responsible, and comply with procedural fairness and due process requirements.

## Section 6.1 Algorithmic impact assessment
<!-- tags: D10 D9 | nodes: P M | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

Completing, approving and publishing the final results of an algorithmic impact assessment in an accessible format on the Open Government Portal prior to the production of any automated decision system. Reviewing, approving, and updating the published algorithmic impact assessment on a scheduled basis, including when the functionality or scope of the automated decision system changes.

## Section 6.2.3 Explanation
<!-- tags: D6 D10 | nodes: SINK P | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

Providing a meaningful explanation to clients of how and why the decision was made. For a decision that results in the denial of a benefit or service, a more detailed plain-language explanation is provided to the client, including the principal factors that led to the output and how that output was used by human officers. Explanations must also inform clients of relevant recourse options.

## Section 6.3.13 Ensuring human involvement
<!-- tags: D8 D10 D9 | nodes: P M | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

Ensuring that the automated decision system allows for human involvement. At higher impact levels the final decision must be made by a human. Decisions cannot be made without having clearly defined human involvement during the decision-making process. Humans review the decisions or recommendations made by the system for accuracy and appropriateness and can intervene.

## Section 6.3.12 Legal services
<!-- tags: D10 D7 | nodes: P | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

Consulting with the department's legal services from the concept stage of an automation project to ensure that the use of the automated decision system is compliant with applicable legal requirements.

## Section 6.4 Recourse
<!-- tags: D6 D10 | nodes: SINK | factor: deployment | domain: gov_services -->

Informing clients of recourse options to challenge the administrative decision. Ensuring that recourse options are timely, effective, and easy to access.
