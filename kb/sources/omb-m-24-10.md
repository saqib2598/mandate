## Rights-impacting uses
<!-- tags: D10 D9 | nodes: P M | factor: standards | domain: gov_services -->

M-24-10 distinguishes safety-impacting and rights-impacting uses of artificial intelligence. A system that helps decide a benefit, a right, or access to a public programme is rights-impacting. Minimum practices include an impact assessment before deployment, testing in the real operational context, and ongoing monitoring after the system is in use.

## Human fallback and incidents
<!-- tags: D13 D8 | nodes: SINK C | factor: deployment | domain: gov_services -->

The memorandum expects a human fallback when the system fails or when a person asks for review of a rights-impacting decision. An outage of a vendor-hosted model does not suspend that duty. The agency still needs a way to continue the service and to report the incident. Data processed in a foreign vendor region remains subject to the agency's residency and privacy review.
