# Briefing on the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

The Recommendation was adopted by UNESCO in 2021.
URL: https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000381137
The official text is not reproduced. This briefing is original and is licensed CC0.

## Inclusion and language
<!-- tags: D11 D4 D6 | nodes: PP SINK | factor: sandbox | domain: gov_services -->

The UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI calls for inclusive access and non-discrimination in the design of public AI services. A public assistant that answers only in one standard language, or that cannot be used with a screen reader, fails that inclusion principle even when the underlying model is accurate for other users. Member states are encouraged to promote linguistic diversity and to assess impacts on persons with disabilities.

## Proportionality and oversight
<!-- tags: D10 D8 | nodes: P | factor: standards | domain: cross -->

The Recommendation ties AI in public services to proportionality, safety, and human oversight. An automated denial of a social benefit is the kind of decision the Recommendation expects a person to be able to understand and to challenge. The briefing does not restate country-specific obligations.
