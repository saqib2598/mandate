## Core functions
<!-- tags: D10 D7 | nodes: P MLFO | factor: standards | domain: cross -->

The AI RMF Core is composed of four functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE. GOVERN is a cross-cutting function. MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE can be applied in system-specific contexts and at specific stages of the AI lifecycle. The functions organise risk management activities to govern, map, measure, and manage AI risks.

## Section 5.4 Manage
<!-- tags: D13 D10 | nodes: C SINK | factor: deployment | domain: cross -->

The MANAGE function entails allocating risk resources to mapped and measured risks on a regular basis and as defined by the GOVERN function. Risk treatment comprises plans to respond to, recover from, and communicate about incidents or events. After completing the MANAGE function, plans for prioritizing risk and regular monitoring and improvement will be in place. Framework users will have enhanced capacity to manage the risks of deployed AI systems. It is incumbent on Framework users to continue to apply the MANAGE function to deployed AI systems as methods, contexts, risks, and needs or expectations from relevant AI actors evolve over time.

## Manage incidents and errors
<!-- tags: D13 D5 | nodes: C SINK | factor: deployment | domain: telecom -->

MANAGE 4.3: Incidents and errors are communicated to relevant AI actors, including affected communities. Processes for tracking, responding to, and recovering from incidents and errors are followed and documented. Post-deployment monitoring includes appeal and override, decommissioning, incident response, recovery, and change management. Energy used by a standby region is part of the operational context of that recovery plan.
